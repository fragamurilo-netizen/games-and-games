TITLE
One More Matchday: Manager

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Be the manager: pick the XI, do deals, play whole seasons. Offline, no timers.

FULL DESCRIPTION
You are the manager. Pick a club, build the team and find out how far you can take it. When the final whistle blows, the question never changes: just one more matchday?

Mais Uma Rodada ("One More Matchday") is a football management sim built for your phone. It plays offline, one-handed, at your own pace. No energy, no timers, no loot boxes. The league table is what keeps you playing.

★ A WHOLE FOOTBALL WORLD
• 980 clubs in 54 leagues across 43 countries, with promotion and relegation
• About 27,000 players with attributes, personality, origin and a career history
• Domestic cups, state championships, super cups and continental tournaments in their current formats, with league phase and knockouts
• National teams with qualifiers, nations leagues, continental tournaments and a world tournament, each with its historical honours

★ MATCHES THAT TELL A STORY
• Minute-by-minute engine: tactics, fatigue, cards, injuries, penalties and stoppage time
• Live 2D match with commentary and scores from around the league, or a quick sim when you only want the result
• Pause to change tactics, give instructions or make a substitution in two taps
• Upsets happen, but the stronger side usually wins, just like real football

★ YOU MAKE THE CALLS
• Pick your XI on the pitch, in your club's shirts, and set the game plan in a few taps
• 8 formations, mentality, style, pressing, defensive line and set pieces
• Weekly training, individual training, team chemistry and squad rotation

★ EVERY PLAYER HAS A STORY
• Star ratings through your staff's eyes: the better your scout, the smaller the doubt
• Profiles with origin, career, past clubs and season-by-season growth
• Youth academy, trials, scouts and prospects who grow into stars

★ TRANSFERS AND THE BOARDROOM
• Bids, counter-offers, swaps, loans and renewals, typing the exact amount you want
• Every club has its own philosophy: some sign only local players, others bet on youth
• Board objectives, fans, press, finances and stadium

★ YOUR LEGACY IS KEPT
• Every club's history, with its titles and the managers of each era
• Trophy room, season awards and Hall of Fame
• 5 save slots and autosave every matchday

★ MAKE IT YOURS
• Editor for clubs, kits, leagues and players
• Mod support to build your own football world

Start for free and play a full season. Enjoying it? Unlock the Full Career once and play as many seasons as you like, with no ads and no subscription.

All players are fictional. Clubs and competitions are inspired by real football, with no affiliation to any official body.
