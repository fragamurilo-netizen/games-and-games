TITLE
One More Matchday: Manager

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Be the manager: pick the XI, do deals, play whole seasons. Offline, no timers.

FULL DESCRIPTION
You are the manager. Pick a club, build the team and find out how far you can take it. When the final whistle blows, the question never changes: just one more matchday?

Mais Uma Rodada ("One More Matchday") is a football management sim built for your phone. It plays offline, in portrait, one-handed. No energy, no timers, no loot boxes. The league table is what keeps you playing.

★ A WHOLE FOOTBALL WORLD
• 670+ clubs in 53 leagues across 43 countries, with promotion and relegation
• About 16,000 players with 15 attributes, potential, personality and a career history
• Domestic cups, super cups and continental tournaments in their real-life formats
• National teams, qualifiers and a world tournament

★ MATCHES THAT TELL A STORY
• Minute-by-minute engine: tactics, fatigue, cards, injuries, penalties and stoppage time
• Live 2D match with commentary, scores from around the league and three speeds
• Pause any time to change tactics or make substitutions
• Upsets happen, but the stronger side usually wins, just like real football

★ YOU MAKE THE CALLS
• 8 formations, mentality, style, pressing, defensive line and set pieces
• An automatic game plan for when you are winning or losing
• Weekly training, team chemistry and squad rotation

★ TRANSFERS AND THE BOARDROOM
• Bids, counter-offers, swaps, loans and renewals
• Every club has its own philosophy: some sign only local players, others bet on youth
• Youth academy, trials and prospects who grow into stars
• Board objectives, fans, press, finances and stadium

★ YOUR LEGACY IS KEPT
• Trophy room, season awards, best-player-in-the-world vote and Hall of Fame
• Full history for every season, every player and your own career
• 5 save slots and autosave every matchday

★ MAKE IT YOURS
• Editor for clubs, kits, leagues and players
• Mod support to build your own football world

Start for free and play a full season. Enjoying it? Unlock the Full Career once and play as many seasons as you like, with no ads and no subscription.

Clubs, players and competitions are fictional or inspired by real football, with no affiliation to any official body.
